What did
vorticity forget?

On a periodic square, adding a constant flow changes how particles wind around the torus but changes no local vorticity. The missing information is exactly two numbers: circulation around the horizontal cycle and circulation around the vertical cycle.

VELOCITY RECONSTRUCTION ON THE FLAT TORUS
velocity equals coexact plus harmonic

The physical coefficients are the real numbers cₓ and cᵧ. Vorticity does not choose them, and there is no preferred “nearest lattice point” in ordinary Clebsch evolution. Quantization appears later as an optional extra experiment.

LOOP DATA

Two noncontractible periods remain.

normalized horizontal and vertical periods

HARMONIC DATA

The periods select one velocity among many.

coexact reconstruction plus two harmonic modes

All choices share the same vorticity. Pressure cannot choose between them because subtracting d p changes no closed-loop period.

WHAT “NEAREST” MEANS HERE
coordinate rounding on this unit flat torus

This minimizes the squared residual norm only because dx and dy form the chosen orthogonal unit basis. Change q, the harmonic basis, or its metric Gram matrix, and “nearest” can change. There is no canonical nearest point before making those choices.

INTEGER FIELD TO SUBTRACT(m,n) = (0,0)

Two different zeros: (m,n) = (0,0) means “remove nothing.” It does not set the physical state c to zero.

What the subtraction means

Three operations that look similar—but are not.

All three manipulate one-forms. Only one is a pressure projection, and only one changes the cohomology class.

01 · PRESSURE

Remove an exact field

eta minus d p

Enforces zero divergence. Closed-loop periods and vorticity are unchanged because an exact form integrates to zero around every closed loop.

02 · STREAMFUNCTION

Recover the coexact field

vorticity maps to curl pseudoinverse

Recovers the least-norm velocity seen by vorticity. “Least norm” silently sets both harmonic periods to zero; it does not infer them.

03 · OPTIONAL QUANTIZATION

Change the harmonic state

subtract a lattice vector

Preserves divergence and vorticity, but changes cross-sectional flux. “Nearest” means minimum residual energy only after this lab imposes q, the basis dx,dy, and the flat metric.

FLUID COHOMOLOGY, YIN ET AL. 2023

The physical coefficients are continuous—and they evolve.

The paper reconstructs velocity as a vorticity-determined part plus real harmonic coefficients. Those coefficients encode cross-sectional flux and obey their own Euler dynamics. This lab freezes one instant and adds an integer lattice solely to make the reconstruction ambiguity explorable.
