06 · PHYSICAL DESTINATION

Fluids on surfaces: a deliberate reading order

These papers do not describe one interchangeable method. They progressively change the stored state, the preserved structure, the topology, and finally the governing equations.

1Choose the stateVelocity, covector, vorticity, or Clebsch variables 2Preserve structureRelabeling, Kelvin circulation, energy, Casimirs 3Restore topologyHarmonic periods that vorticity does not determine 4Add surface physicsDepth, curvature, boundaries, and viscosity
1 · REPRESENTATION

What should the solver carry?

Read these after the gentle Clebsch introduction. They explain why circulation is naturally a one-form measurement, and why Clebsch variables are a factorization of that one-form rather than a new physical fluid.

ACM TOG · 2017Chern, Knöppel, Pinkall & Schröder

Inside Fluids: Clebsch Maps for Visualization and Processing

Uses Clebsch maps to expose vortex structure and make a three-dimensional velocity field accessible through transported scalar data.

Project + paper ↗See the scalar factorization →
ACM TOG · 2022Nabizadeh, Wang, Ramamoorthi & Chern

Covector Fluids

Rewrites Euler advection in covector form so the numerical update follows circulation and Kelvin’s theorem more directly than ordinary velocity interpolation.

Project + paper ↗Covector lesson →
ACM TOG · 2023Yin, Nabizadeh, Wu, Wang & Chern

Fluid Cohomology

Derives the missing evolution of harmonic velocity components on domains with holes, periodic boundaries, or genus. This is the research foundation for the torus lesson.

Paper ↗Interactive companion ↗Torus lab →
2 · VARIATIONAL STRUCTURE

Which invariants survive discretization?

The older work establishes the discrete-diffeomorphism and Lagrange–d’Alembert lineage. The new vakonomic formulation makes a different choice for nonholonomic constraints and supplies the clearest research destination for low-rank Clebsch state.

PHYSICA D · 2011Pavlov, Mullen, Tong, Kanso, Marsden & Desbrun

Structure-Preserving Discretization of Incompressible Fluids

Approximates volume-preserving diffeomorphisms by a finite-dimensional Lie group and derives a discrete Eulerian integrator with a discrete Kelvin theorem.

Read the paper ↗Course bridge →
PREPRINT · 2026Roy-Chowdhury, Nabizadeh, Gross, Gruber & Chern

Vakonomic Fluids

Treats the discrete velocity constraint vakonomically, yielding Lie–Poisson dynamics, a discrete relabeling symmetry, Kelvin circulation, and a low-rank momentum-map representation analogous to Clebsch variables.

Preprint ↗Representation prerequisite →
DCDS · 2014Desbrun, Gawlik, Gay-Balmaz & Zeitlin

Variational Discretization for Rotating Stratified Fluids

Extends the geometric integrator viewpoint to advected density and rotation, preserving a discrete Kelvin theorem and long-term energy behavior. It is a useful bridge toward geophysical shallow-water structure.

Paper ↗Shallow-water destination →
3 · TOPOLOGY

Vorticity is local; periods are global.

The annulus lab introduces one missing circulation. The flat-torus lab exposes two. Return to Fluid Cohomology above for the equations that evolve those harmonic coefficients rather than freezing them.

4 · SURFACE PHYSICS

What changes when the domain bends—or the depth evolves?

Shallow water replaces the incompressible horizontal constraint by depth-weighted continuity. Viscous surface flow adds a separate geometric burden: curvature, boundary conditions, and harmonic-mode dynamics enter the dissipation.

SCA · 2007Wang, Miller & Turk

Solving General Shallow Wave Equations on Surfaces

A graphics baseline for shallow water directly on curved surfaces without requiring one global parameterization.

Project + paper ↗Run the baseline →
ACM TOG · 2025Zhu, Yin & Chern

Viscous Vortex Dynamics on Surfaces

Derives curvature-dependent viscous terms and the evolution of harmonic components on surfaces of arbitrary topology, including non-orientable domains and several boundary conditions.

Paper ↗Author preprint ↗Boundary prerequisite →
