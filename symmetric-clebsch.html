The conclusion

Symmetric labels are a convenient gauge choice, not the definition of a symmetric Clebsch field. If the labels look asymmetric, assemble the one-form before declaring that the physics broke symmetry.

Orientation-reversing symmetry

should change sign.

The vorticity itself is the two-form omega equals d u flat. A mirror reverses the planar area form, so the scalar coefficient used to color that two-form is odd even when the physical two-form is invariant.
