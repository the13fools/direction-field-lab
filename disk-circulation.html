The lesson

A nonzero boundary circulation on the full disk is not a hidden harmonic degree of freedom. Either the interior carries the corresponding vorticity, or the field ceases to be smooth somewhere. Removing the center turns the disk into an annulus and creates the missing noncontractible loop.
