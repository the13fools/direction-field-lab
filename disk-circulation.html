The lesson

A nonzero boundary circulation on the full disk is not a hidden harmonic degree of freedom. On an annulus, however, the circulation difference is fixed by vorticity while one common circulation remains harmonic. Removing the center creates exactly one noncontractible loop.

Want closed walls instead?

Choose one streamfunction value on every physical boundary piece. A smooth example for these horizontal slits is one minus radius squared times imaginary z. It vanishes on both slit shores and the outer circle, so star d psi wall creates vortical recirculation with no boundary flux.

03 · THE ANSWER

A branch cut is bookkeeping—not an annular hole.

disk versus punctured disk cohomology

Glue the slit shores and u becomes multivalued on the original disk. Treat the slits as physical walls and the boundary-attached slit domain is still simply connected. Only removing a compact center creates the annulus and its genuine circulation degree of freedom.
