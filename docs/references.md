# Canonical reading map

The live version of this reading path is [`references.html`](../references.html). It explains which idea in the lab each source supports. For a report or paper, follow the project or DOI link and use the authors' preferred BibTeX.

## Discrete forms and Hodge theory

- Keenan Crane, Fernando de Goes, Mathieu Desbrun, and Peter Schröder. *Digital Geometry Processing with Discrete Exterior Calculus*. SIGGRAPH Courses, 2013. [Course and notes](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/Projects/DDG/)
- Fernando de Goes, Mathieu Desbrun, and Yiying Tong. *Vector Field Processing on Triangle Meshes*. SIGGRAPH Courses, 2016. [Course notes](https://www.geometry.caltech.edu/pubs/dGDT15.pdf)

## Direction fields, connections, and integrability

- Keenan Crane, Mathieu Desbrun, and Peter Schröder. *Trivial Connections on Discrete Surfaces*. Computer Graphics Forum, 2010. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8659.2010.01761.x)
- Felix Knöppel, Keenan Crane, Ulrich Pinkall, and Peter Schröder. *Globally Optimal Direction Fields*. ACM Transactions on Graphics, 2013. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/2461912.2462005)
- Olga Diamanti, Amir Vaxman, Daniele Panozzo, and Olga Sorkine-Hornung. *Integrable PolyVector Fields*. ACM Transactions on Graphics, 2015. [Project and paper](https://igl.ethz.ch/projects/integrable/)
- Beibei Liu, Yiying Tong, Fernando de Goes, and Mathieu Desbrun. *Discrete Connection and Covariant Derivative for Vector Field Analysis and Design*. ACM Transactions on Graphics, 2016. [Paper](https://geometry.caltech.edu/pubs/LTDD14.pdf)
- Amir Vaxman et al. *Directional: A Library for Directional Field Synthesis, Design, and Processing*. [Tutorial](https://avaxman.github.io/Directional/tutorial/)

## Periodic and geodesic stripe realization

- Felix Knöppel, Keenan Crane, Ulrich Pinkall, and Peter Schröder. *Stripe Patterns on Surfaces*. ACM Transactions on Graphics, 2015. [Project and paper](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/Projects/StripePatterns/)
- Josh Vekhter, Jiacheng Zhuo, Luisa F. Gil Fandino, Qixing Huang, and Etienne Vouga. *Weaving Geodesic Foliations*. ACM Transactions on Graphics, 2019. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3306346.3323043)
- Juan Montes Maestre, Yinwei Du, Ronan Hinchet, Stelian Coros, and Bernhard Thomaszewski. *Differentiable Stripe Patterns for Inverse Design of Structured Surfaces*. ACM Transactions on Graphics, 2023. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3592114)

## Differentiation and surface flow

- Patrick Schmidt, Janis Born, David Bommes, Marcel Campen, and Leif Kobbelt. *TinyAD: Automatic Differentiation in Geometry Processing Made Simple*. Computer Graphics Forum, 2022. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/cgf.14607) · [Code](https://github.com/patr-schm/TinyAD)
- William S. Moses and Valentin Churavy. *Instead of Rewriting Foreign Code for Machine Learning, Automatically Synthesize Fast Gradients*. NeurIPS, 2020. [Enzyme project](https://enzyme.mit.edu/)
- Huamin Wang, Gavin Miller, and Greg Turk. *Solving General Shallow Wave Equations on Surfaces*. Symposium on Computer Animation, 2007. [Project and paper](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~turk/paper_pages/2007_shallow_waves/index.html)
