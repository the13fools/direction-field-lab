FIELD CONSTRUCTION
Why distinguish curl-free and divergence-free?

grad φ is curl-free but can create sources and sinks. Rotating it gives J grad ψ, which is divergence-free but generally has vorticity. They are the exact and coexact sides of the surface Hodge decomposition.

Where does Perlin noise enter?

The spatial directions and phases are seeded once. Every modal amplitude and phase then follows its own smooth one-dimensional gradient-noise path in time. Projection is spatial, so it remains valid at every instant.

What do the Clebsch fields mean?

The three scalar fields φ, α, and β receive independent multiscale time noise. The raw field can compress. The projected mode solves Δψ = ωC and displays the coexact field J grad ψ.

01 · TIME NOISE

Randomness moves without popping.

Seeded gradient noise supplies a smooth coefficient path for every spatial mode. Coarse bands turn over slowly; finer bands evolve faster, while a zero rate recovers a frozen field.

Vorticity becomes the crossing of two scalar fields.

For u♭ = dφ + α dβ, exterior differentiation removes the exact part and leaves du♭ = dα ∧ dβ. The live vorticity layer shows that two-form on the surface.
