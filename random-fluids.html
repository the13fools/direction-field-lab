FIELD CONSTRUCTION
Why distinguish curl-free and divergence-free?

grad φ is curl-free but can create sources and sinks. Rotating it gives J grad ψ, which is divergence-free but generally has vorticity. They are the exact and coexact sides of the surface Hodge decomposition.

Where does Perlin noise enter?

The spatial directions and phases are seeded once. Every modal amplitude and phase then follows its own smooth one-dimensional gradient-noise path in time. Projection is spatial, so it remains valid at every instant.

What do the Clebsch fields mean?

The three scalar fields φ, α, and β receive independent multiscale time noise. The term α dβ contributes vorticity dα ∧ dβ, shown by the magenta/cyan surface layer.

Randomness moves without popping.
