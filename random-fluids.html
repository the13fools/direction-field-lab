SURFACE
Why no sources or sinks?

On an oriented surface, J rotates a tangent vector by ninety degrees. Applying it to grad ψ turns level-set normals into level-set tangents, and the resulting Hamiltonian field preserves surface area.

What is random here?

The mode directions, phases, signs, and turnover directions are seeded. The amplitude of band k is chosen so the velocity energy falls approximately like k−β.
