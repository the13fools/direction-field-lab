THE REPRESENTATION
u flat = d phi + sum lambda a d B a
velocity vector = metric raised one-form

Here B = (X,Y,Z). On a triangle mesh these are initially the three coordinates of each vertex. On this periodic square we use the coordinates of a standard embedded torus, so no label jumps across a seam.

HEIGHT / DENSITYh(x,y,t)

Water column carried by the flux hu. Pressure comes from its gradient.

BERNOULLI POTENTIALφ(x,y,t)

Its differential dφ supplies the exact, curl-free part of the velocity one-form.

THREE LABEL WEIGHTSλ(x,y,t) ∈ ℝ³

Three transported coefficients weight the differentials of the ambient labels.

THREE MATERIAL LABELSB(x,y,t) ∈ ℝ³

Initially vertex XYZ. Their level sets move with the water until we deliberately rechart.

Why combine these ideas?

Shallow water gives the labels a physical job to do.

The random-field playground can show Clebsch assembly and pressure projection without choosing a time evolution. Here the labels travel with an actual layer: velocity moves them, height changes under mass flux, and the Bernoulli potential responds to gravity.

FOR OCEAN-LIKE FLOWSMaterial structure matters.

Vorticity and tracers can persist as water moves over long distances; a label-based state makes that material viewpoint explicit.

FOR CURVED SURFACESThe representation separates roles.

One-forms measure circulation, vectors move parcels, and the metric converts between them—useful bookkeeping before moving from a plane to a sphere or mesh.

FOR PERIODIC OR HOLED DOMAINSLocal labels are not the whole state.

Global harmonic periods still have to be stored or evolved. This flat example sets them to zero so the local transport can be read first.

THE IMPORTANT CHANGE OF PROBLEM

Do not project shallow water to zero divergence.

In the random-flow lab, subtracting d p is a useful incompressible comparison. In shallow water, convergence and divergence are physical: they raise and lower the water column. Pressure acts through the evolution of φ while the continuity equation evolves h.

height changes with mass-flux divergence
pressure enters the Bernoulli potential

On a torus, the velocity state may also carry two harmonic periods. This planar lab sets them to zero; a topology-aware solver must retain and audit them separately.

THE NUMERICAL REPAIR

Rechart the representation, not the fluid.

Transport eventually distorts any finite-resolution label grid. At a rechart time, the solver first saves the current one-form, resets B to the ambient XYZ coordinate functions, and solves for new weights λ. Height, material dye, and physical time do not reset.

save the non-exact part and reset the labels
minimum norm weights preserve the residual one-form

The “rechart defect” below is the relative RMS change in velocity caused by this discrete solve. It should remain near machine precision immediately after a reset. This is a gauge change, not a pressure projection and not remeshing.

What this prototype actually claims

A readable numerical experiment, not yet a production surface solver.

The lab couples a conservative periodic height update to semi-Lagrangian Clebsch transport. It is useful for seeing the variables and the recharting problem. It should not be read as evidence that all of the geometric and conservation issues are finished.

IMPLEMENTED HEREA periodic shallow-water layer.

Height obeys a conservative face-flux update. The Clebsch labels and dye follow the reconstructed velocity, φ receives the pressure term, and mass and representation defects are measured.

DELIBERATELY OMITTEDThe hard surface cases.

No curved triangle mesh, bathymetry, Coriolis force, wet/dry front, shock treatment, viscosity, or nonzero harmonic circulation appears in this square experiment.

FUTURE SOLVER WORKPreserve more than a picture.

A serious mesh version needs DEC/FEM mass matrices, a coupled conservative time integrator, topology-aware harmonic state, robust chart resets, and benchmark tests for energy, potential vorticity, and Kelvin circulation.

So which things are vectors?

u and the mass flux hu are vectors. The displayed bars visualize the covectors dφ and ΣλₐdBᵃ. Each Bᵃ and λₐ is still a scalar field; the three-component bookkeeping index is not a tangent-vector index.

Why draw a covector as bars?

A covector assigns a number to every test displacement. Its kernel is the line of displacements on which that number is zero. Parallel bars make that kernel visible; their normal points in the measuring direction.

Does XYZ mean the velocity lives in ℝ³?

No. The label map uses three ambient scalar functions, but each differential is restricted to the two-dimensional tangent plane. The reconstructed one-form is tangent. On a mesh, discrete gradients of vertex XYZ supply a redundant spanning frame on each face.

What is still omitted?

This periodic example fixes both harmonic circulations to zero. Vorticity cannot reconstruct those two torus periods; a complete torus solver must store or evolve them. A real surface solver also needs mesh mass matrices, intrinsic gradient/divergence operators, tangent transport, and conservative wet/dry handling.

The careful version

Scalar channels → one-form → vector → transport → rechart.

WHERE DOES ROTATION COME FROM?

The crossing of two scalar foliations.

vorticity two-form = sum d lambda a wedge d B a

The exact piece disappears because d squared phi is zero. Each channel contributes a two-form; on an oriented surface this can also be displayed as a signed scalar density or a normal pseudovector.

VECTOR VS. COVECTOR

Same two numbers here; different jobs.

vector u

Consumes time and produces displacement.

covector u♭

Consumes displacement and produces a number.

In Cartesian coordinates with the identity metric, lowering an index copies the components. On a curved or stretched mesh, the metric changes them.

THE SHALLOW-WATER SYSTEM IN GENERALIZED CLEBSCH VARIABLES

The labels move; height compresses; φ pays the Bernoulli bill.

D t is the material derivative. The lab semi-Lagrangian-advects all three B and λ channels, advances h with conservative periodic face fluxes, and evolves φ by a Bernoulli equation. Weak filtering handles unresolved grid scales; recharting separately repairs a distorted representation while auditing the velocity it preserves.

  1. lambda and B channels are material invariants

    Between rechart events, all six scalar channels move with the parcels.

  2. Bernoulli evolution of phi

    Kinetic and pressure terms update the exact potential.

  3. mass continuity

    Height is a density, so it changes under compression.

  4. reconstruct velocity

    The one-form is rebuilt from the exact part and redundant label frame.

  5. rechart with the pseudoinverse frame

    Reset the labels to ambient coordinates and refit only their weights.
