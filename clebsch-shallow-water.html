So which things are vectors?

u and the mass flux hu are vectors. The gradients drawn as arrows are metric-dual vectors used to visualize the covectors dφ and dβ. The primitive Clebsch variables themselves are scalars.

Why draw a covector as bars?

A covector assigns a number to every test displacement. Its kernel is the line of displacements on which that number is zero. Parallel bars make that kernel visible; their normal points in the measuring direction.

What is omitted?

This periodic example has zero prescribed harmonic circulation. On a torus, two additional global periods may be needed. On a curved surface, the metric-dependent sharp map is no longer visually invisible.

WHERE DOES ROTATION COME FROM?

The crossing of two scalar foliations.

vorticity two-form = d alpha wedge d beta

The exact piece disappears because d squared phi is zero. Rotation is large where α and β change strongly in independent directions. If their contours are parallel, their wedge product vanishes.

VECTOR VS. COVECTOR

Same two numbers here; different jobs.

vector u

Consumes time and produces displacement.

covector u♭

Consumes displacement and produces a number.

In Cartesian coordinates with the identity metric, lowering an index copies the components. On a curved or stretched mesh, the metric changes them.
