An arrow
and a measurement.

A tangent vector v in the tangent space is an arrow: it can be a particle's instantaneous motion. A covector eta in the cotangent space is a linear measuring device: feed it an arrow and it returns one number.

a covector evaluates a tangent vector

For a fluid, the metric converts the velocity arrow u into the velocity one-form eta equals u flat. Locally it measures a tangent direction; along a whole curve it measures circulation.

the velocity one-form integrated along a curve

Only now do Clebsch variables enter: u flat equals d phi plus alpha d beta. They parameterize the one-form; they are not themselves the velocity, an evolution law, or an incompressibility condition.

Orient yourself before the details

One fluid. Several ways to store it.

The physical problem comes first: find an incompressible velocity that moves particles and carries the desired rotation. Velocity, vorticity, and Clebsch variables are competing descriptions of that state—not different kinds of fluid.

THE SOLVER MUST EVENTUALLY PRODUCE
move particles with a divergence-free velocity

Whatever the solver stores internally, particle advection ultimately needs the vector u. The other representations reorganize information that helps construct or evolve it.

The key distinction

Vorticity determines the rotational part of velocity, but not the exact or harmonic parts. Clebsch supplies a candidate velocity; Hodge projection fixes its exact part. Starting from vorticity alone requires the same kind of global reconstruction before particles can move.

WHY ONE GLOBAL φ IS IMPOSSIBLE

Exact forms have zero period.

closed-loop contradiction

A single-valued scalar returns to its starting value after a closed loop. The toroidal circulation does not. Therefore c d theta is closed but not globally exact.

HOW THE EXTRA CHART FIXES IT

Move the cuts and glue derivatives.

Chart A fails only at one meridian; chart B puts its cut at another. On overlaps their angle values differ by a constant, so their differentials—and hence the velocity—agree exactly.

WHAT A SOLVER USUALLY STORES

Keep topology as a harmonic coefficient.

Hodge decomposition on the torus

Rather than manage multivalued potentials, a mesh solver can store two global harmonic basis one-forms and their circulation coefficients. Charts explain the geometry; harmonic modes are often the cleaner numerical representation.

The distinction between closed and exact forms is the de Rham-cohomology obstruction. The torus has two independent degree-one classes, corresponding to its two noncontractible cycles; see the MIT notes on de Rham cohomology and the Hodge Laplacian. Notes-guided path · Chapters 1, 2, and 5

From arrows to circulation.

The course notes build exterior calculus in this order: distinguish vectors from covectors; interpret forms by what they integrate over; introduce d, wedge, and Stokes; add the metric and Hodge star; then treat fluid velocity as a one-form. The Clebsch formula is our worked example in that language.

0
DUAL PAIRING

An arrow is not a covector.

A vector v is a possible tangent displacement or velocity. A covector eta at p is a linear function of such displacements. Picture the vector as an arrow and the covector as a stack of local contour lines: the pairing counts signed contour crossings.

NO METRIC REQUIRED
a covector maps an arrow to a scalar
This is a dual pairing, not a dot product. A metric is extra structure and is not needed merely to evaluate η on v.
1
MICROSCOPIC ↔ MACROSCOPIC

A one-form has two compatible pictures.

Microscopically, eta at p acting on v measures one tangent arrow. Macroscopically, the integral of eta along gamma adds those infinitesimal measurements along an oriented curve. For fluid velocity this integral is circulation.

FROM LOCAL READINGS TO A CURVE
integrate a one-form along a parameterized curve
DEC stores this macroscopic quantity on an edge: the edge number approximates ∫e η. It is not saying that the smooth one-form exists only on edges.

CHOOSE THREE 0-FORMS

Paint three scalar fields.

The fields phi, alpha, and beta map the surface to real numbers. They are not vectors or coordinates that the surface must obey. In this lab α and β are label fields; φ supplies an exact potential contribution.

the three controlled scalar fields
At θ = 0, β changes in the same direction as α. At θ = π/2, their changes are independent.
3
EXTERIOR DERIVATIVE

A scalar derivative is a one-form.

For a tangent displacement v, the number d alpha of v is the instantaneous change of α along v. This definition needs no metric. Only after applying sharp g may the lab draw a gradient arrow.

COORDINATE CALCULATION
differentiate the two labels
On the Euclidean plane, (dα)♯ = ∇α. On a curved surface, the metric changes the conversion from covector to vector.
4
WEDGE + STOKES

A two-form measures oriented patches.

The value d alpha wedge d beta evaluated on v and w is the signed determinant of the label changes along two tangent directions. For velocity, the vorticity two-form d u flat measures the circulation around the patch boundary.

THE LOCAL AND INTEGRAL PICTURES AGREE
Stokes and the planar Clebsch vorticity
At θ = 0 the two label differentials are parallel, so their determinant and the resolved circulation density both vanish.

CLEBSCH PARAMETERIZATION

Now assemble the velocity one-form.

The scalar α scales the one-form d beta pointwise. Adding d phi gives a velocity covector. The surface metric then supplies the velocity arrow that actually transports a particle.

WHY THE VORTICITY IS SIMPLE
assemble, raise the index, and differentiate
The identity d² = 0 removes the exact terms. On a closed loop, ∮dφ = 0 as well: the potential changes local velocity but not closed-loop circulation.

The whole story in one sentence

A velocity arrow moves a particle; its metric-dual one-form measures circulation; Clebsch variables parameterize that one-form; d turns it into vorticity; and a separate, metric-dependent Hodge projection enforces zero divergence.

This route follows Stephanie Wang, Mohammad Sina Nabizadeh, and Albert Chern, Exterior Calculus in Graphics: Course Notes for a SIGGRAPH 2023 Course, especially Chapters 1, 2, and 5. The notes provide the vector/covector and fluid-form language; this lab then applies that language to the Clebsch parameterization.

Smooth form first · discrete cochain second

What the mesh stores.

A smooth form is defined all over the surface by what it measures. DEC stores a finite set of those measurements as cochains: point samples on vertices, line integrals on oriented edges, and area integrals on oriented faces.

01 · 0-FORM SAMPLE

Evaluate α at mesh vertices

The smooth scalar exists between vertices too. Its discrete 0-cochain keeps only the sampled values α(vi).

02 · 1-FORM INTEGRAL

Integrate dα along each edge

On edge i → j, the integral of d alpha is alpha j minus alpha i. Reversing orientation reverses the sign.

03 · 2-FORM INTEGRAL

Integrate vorticity over a face

The face cochain stores the integral of d alpha wedge d beta over f: the signed circulation around that face.

04 · VELOCITY CIRCULATION

Measure u♭ along each edge

A discrete one-form stores line integrals, not vertex arrows. The metric and sharp g are needed to reconstruct a tangent velocity vector for drawing or advection.

05 · HODGE PROJECTION

Remove the exact 1-form dp

Solve Laplacian p = divergence u flat, then subtract d p. Curl is unchanged because d squared p is zero.

This smooth-to-discrete distinction follows the integration viewpoint in Exterior Calculus in Graphics and the primal-cochain implementation viewpoint in the Geometry Collective’s Discrete Exterior Calculus project.

Now compare the implementation choices

What does each representation buy?

The worked rotation above showed that these encodings can describe the same fluid. Here is the practical reason a simulator might choose one over another.

1
VELOCITY OR VELOCITY COVECTOR

Store what moves the particles.

This is the most direct state. The vector u advects particles; the one-form eta equals u flat measures circulation. Pressure projection is straightforward.

BEST FEATURE

No reconstruction is needed before advection.

COST

Naively advecting vector components can numerically damp or distort vorticity. Covector pullback transport is designed to reduce this problem.

2
VORTICITY TWO-FORM

Store the rotational content directly.

On a surface, omega equals d eta is one scalar density times oriented area. It is the natural state if vortex transport is the main concern.

BEST FEATURE

Rotation is represented directly and is not hidden inside derivatives of velocity.

COST

Vorticity cannot move particles by itself. At every step, recover a velocity by solving d eta equals omega and delta eta equals zero, together with boundary conditions and harmonic circulation periods.

3
CLEBSCH POTENTIALS

Factor vorticity into two scalar labels.

Clebsch candidate and its vorticity

Think of α and β as two painted coordinate grids carried by the fluid. Where their contours cross determines vorticity. This label-based picture is useful in some variational and Hamiltonian schemes.

BEST FEATURE

The identity d eta C equals d alpha wedge d beta builds curl compatibility into the representation.

COST

The potentials are nonunique, may need multiple charts, and the raw candidate eta C is generally not incompressible.

The direct answer

What does Hodge-projecting Clebsch buy?

  1. START WITH A VORTICAL CANDIDATE

    Clebsch supplies vorticity, not incompressibility.

    The one-form eta C already has the desired vorticity omega, but it may contain an exact source–sink component.

    raw Clebsch properties
  2. REMOVE ONLY THE SOURCE–SINK PART

    Solve one scalar Poisson problem.

    Find p so that d p has exactly the same divergence as the candidate, then subtract it. In Clebsch language this simply replaces φ by phi minus p; α, β, and their vorticity do not change.

    Hodge projection chooses the exact potential
  3. KEEP THE ROTATION

    The result is a usable incompressible velocity.

    The projection changes the local arrows, but it changes neither the vorticity nor the closed-loop circulation periods of the candidate.

    projected properties
Projection is not extra fluid physics.

It selects the exact potential φ that makes the Clebsch candidate divergence-free. If you evolve vorticity directly, you still need a global solve for d eta equals omega and delta eta equals zero before moving particles. Clebsch plus projection is worthwhile when the moving labels α and β are useful—not because projection is a cheaper way to recover velocity.

A practical decision

Which representation should the solver use?

Velocity or covector
Use it when particle motion, forces, boundaries, and compatibility with standard pressure solvers matter most. Direct covector transport is often the simplest circulation-aware compromise.
Vorticity two-form
Use it when vortex evolution is primary and the cost of reconstructing velocity through a Poisson/Biot–Savart solve is acceptable.
Clebsch plus projection
Use it when material labels, canonical variables, a variational discretization, or the factorization omega equals d alpha wedge d beta is itself useful. It is not automatically cheaper than the other choices.

Where does the metric enter?

The metric is not needed for d, wedge products, or pullback. It is needed to turn a covector into a vector, turn a vorticity two-form into a displayed scalar, and define divergence:

How many degrees of freedom do Clebsch variables add?

None physically. With a fixed metric, u corresponds to eta uniquely. A surface one-form has two local component functions, whereas Clebsch uses three nonunique potentials. For any smooth F,

Clebsch gauge transformation
the physical one-form is unchanged

The representation is therefore an overcomplete encoding with gauge freedom, not a larger physical state.

What can fail globally on a surface?

Locally, factor omega; then u flat minus alpha d beta is closed and is locally d phi. Globally, topology can contribute harmonic circulation:

Hodge decomposition on a closed genus-g surface

A single globally single-valued Clebsch pair may need to be supplemented by multiple charts, multiple pairs, or an explicit harmonic component.
