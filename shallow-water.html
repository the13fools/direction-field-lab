Why ocean modelers begin here

The ocean is deep to us, but thin compared with a planet.

When the vertical scale is small relative to the horizontal scale, depth-averaged equations capture long waves and large horizontal currents without resolving the full three-dimensional water column.

WAVESHeight and velocity talk to each other.

A raised surface creates a pressure gradient; the resulting flow redistributes the water.

Convergence piles water up and divergence draws it down, so incompressible projection would erase the mechanism we want to study.

What is vertex-based integrability here?

The velocity update is assembled from a scalar vertex height through G h. On this periodic grid the discrete mixed derivatives commute, so the curl of that pressure increment is zero. The displayed curl RMS is the certificate.

What is still missing?

A triangle surface needs metric mass matrices, tangent-frame transport, mesh-aware gradient/divergence, a time integrator study, and physical advection of the water state. The dye is only a diagnostic. Curved geometry should replace this baseline only after the same invariants pass.
